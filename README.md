# exitless.org

**This company is not for sale.** Not now, not after the next round, not when the number gets interesting.

Exitless is a small institution from the future, part of [Worth Building](https://worthbuilding.org): perpetual-purpose ownership made physical. The page is the acquisition offer you can only decline. It escalates, and it keeps escalating, until it arrives at the only honest answer: *there is no number.*

Live at [exitless.org](https://exitless.org).

## What is here

- `index.html`: the whole site. One file, no build step, no dependencies beyond two Google Fonts. Open it in a browser.

## Running it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
# then open http://localhost:8000
```

## The idea

A company that cannot be sold makes different decisions. It can keep a promise longer than a quarter. Not because its people are saints, but because its ownership makes the short move structurally impossible. Steward-ownership, perpetual purpose trusts, golden shares held by a foundation: the legal forms exist. This page is about what they feel like from the inside.

A future worth building.
